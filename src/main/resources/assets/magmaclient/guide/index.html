Welcome to Magma

Magma is a helper client for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge and Fabric). It mines, travels, builds and keeps you safe for you. This page explains every feature in plain words — search at the top right, or pick a topic on the left.

  1. In a world, press Right Shift to open the Magma menu.
  2. Click a feature to switch it on. Right-click it (or press the small ▾) to see its settings.
  3. The first time you switch something on, a card in the bottom-right explains how to use it. If a feature gets stuck, another card tells you what to do.
  4. This guide is always one click away: the ? Guide item in the menu sidebar (under Settings), or type .guide in chat.
  5. The Home page shows what's running, progress bars (building, trips, ores) and big buttons for the most common jobs.
Everything only runs on your computer. This guide and Builder Studio are little websites inside the game — they're only reachable from this PC.

Middle-click a featureGive it a key, then press the key you want. Delete or Backspace removes the key, Esc cancels.

Settings (bottom of the sidebar) has the menu colours, Tips on/off, Advanced Settings (shows every fine-tuning option) and updates.

Notifications & tips

Cards appear in the bottom-right corner. The coloured bar tells you the kind: orange = information, green = done, yellow = needs your attention, red = problem. The thin line at the bottom shows how long the card stays.

A card that shows a running job (like "Getting materials") updates itself instead of stacking up.

Don't want the help cards? Menu → Settings → Tips off. Want the "how to use" cards again? Show Guides Again → Reset.

Builder

Builder builds a schematic for you: it scaffolds, places every block facing the right way, clears blocks that are in the way and cleans up after itself.

  1. Get a schematic. Switch Builder on — Builder Studio opens in your browser by itself. Turn a picture into a build, or design one in 3D, then press Send to Minecraft. You can also drop .schem, .litematic or .nbt files into the schematics folder (Builder settings → Schematics → Open folder) and type the name in the Schematic box.
  2. Put it where you want. It appears in front of you as see-through blocks. Hold a stick: right-click moves it to where you look, left-click turns it, arrow keys nudge it, Page Up/Down raise or lower it (Shift + scroll works too).
  3. (Survival) Add a supply chest. Put your building blocks in a chest. In Builder settings press Pick a chest — the menu hides — and right-click the chest. It gets an orange outline. When a block runs out, Builder walks there, takes more and carries on.
  4. Start. Press Start in Builder's settings (or type .build start). Watch progress at the top of the screen and on the Home page.

When it runs out of blocks

It checks your supply chests first. If they're empty too, it pauses and keeps the schematic where it is. Put the missing blocks in your inventory and it carries on by itself (it also re-checks your supply chests once a minute).

Blocks in the way

Wrong blocks where the build goes are outlined red and get cleared first — from the outside in, so it never has to dig blindly. If the build is buried deep in a hill, raise it with the stick + Page Up.

Creative vs survival

In creative it flies to each spot and goes over obstacles. In survival it walks, pillars and bridges with junk blocks (cobblestone, dirt, netherrack) — carry some for tall builds.

Builder commands: .build <name> load · .build start · .build stop pause · .build clear remove · .build here move in front of you · .build rotate · .build chest add supply chest · .build chests clear · .build editor open Studio · .build list · .build folder

Mining

AutoMiner K

Pick the ores you want in its settings and switch it on. It finds them (like X-Ray), digs a safe route to them, mines the whole vein and picks up the drops. It never breaks a block next to lava, walls off creepers and pauses when your health is low. Move yourself and it pauses until you let go.

Not finding anything? It sees about 32 blocks around. Pick more ores, or go deeper — diamonds are most common around Y -59.

X-Ray X

ESP outlines ores through walls. Classic hides every other block so only ores (and lava) are left. Both does both. Pick which ores and how far in settings. Classic works with Sodium; with Embeddium only ESP works.

VeinMiner

Mine one ore or log and the rest of the connected vein breaks too.

FastBreak · AutoTool

FastBreak mines faster (lower the speed if a server complains). AutoTool switches to the best tool for the block you start mining.

LavaGuard L

Your safety net: warns about lava nearby and refuses to break a block that would let lava out. Leave it on.

Travel

Goto

Type X Y Z in its settings, or .goto x y z in chat, then switch it on. It walks, bridges, pillars and digs there safely. The Use buttons fill in your Base, your last death or where you're standing. Exact Height off (recommended) lands you on safe ground at that X/Z.

Base

Press Set here where your base is. Switch Base on any time (or type .base) to walk home.

AutoFind

Pick a structure (Village, Fortress, Stronghold, Ancient City…) and switch it on. It scans what's loaded, explores outward if needed, then walks you there. Fortresses and bastions are only in the Nether; villages, strongholds and monuments only in the Overworld.

Scaffold · SafeWalk · NoFall

Scaffold places blocks under you as you walk (keep blocks in your hotbar). SafeWalk stops you walking off edges, into lava or fire. NoFall removes fall damage.

AutoWalk · Fly · Freecam

AutoWalk keeps walking forward. Fly lets you fly (Space up, sneak down). Freecam lets your camera float while your player stays put.

Villagers — VillagerRoller

  1. Find a villager without a job (or one you haven't traded with) and stand within 3 blocks.
  2. Keep its job block in your inventory — lectern for librarians, composter for farmers, and so on (any job works).
  3. In VillagerRoller settings pick the Books Wanted and/or Items Wanted, the Max Price and whether you want max level only.
  4. Switch it on. It breaks and re-places the job block until the villager offers what you want, then stops and tells you.
Once you trade with a villager its trades are locked forever — roll first, trade after.

Combat

AutoCombat

Fights the nearest enemy you can see: steps in when your attack is fully charged, hits at the edge of reach, then backs out of their reach and circles. Choose Players and/or Monsters in settings; turn off Move For Me if you only want it to aim and hit.

PlayerESP

Boxes around other players through walls, coloured by distance. Most useful on servers.

Utility & vision

AutoEat

Eats from your hotbar or inventory when you're hungry or hurt.

InvCleaner

Throws away the junk items you pick, so your inventory doesn't fill up while mining.

Fullbright

See in the dark (only on your screen).

FastPlace

Places blocks faster while you hold right-click.

HUD

The overlay: Magma watermark with FPS and ping, the list of what's on, your coordinates (with the Nether/Overworld conversion) and a card while AutoMiner or a trip runs. It's also on the title screen. Pick what shows in its settings.

Fixing problems

Builder Studio or this guide didn't open

Open localhost:25590 (Studio) or localhost:25591 (Guide) in your browser yourself. They only work while the game is running.

X-Ray Classic shows nothing different

Classic needs Sodium or vanilla rendering; with Embeddium it switches to ESP automatically. After changing ore lists the world redraws by itself — give it a second.

Builder seems stuck

Look at the top panel: it says what it's doing. "Waiting for materials" → add blocks or a supply chest. "Can't get near …" → the spot is buried; it digs from the outside in, or raise the build with the stick. Still stuck: .build stop then .build start.

AutoMiner / Goto doesn't move

Let go of the movement keys (moving yourself pauses it). If it says "Route locked", move a few blocks or turn on Dig Through.

A server kicks me

Turn down FastBreak speed, turn off Fly and NoFall — many servers check for those.

Updates & installing

Magma checks GitHub for new versions when the game starts. With Auto Update on, new versions download by themselves and install when you restart. On the title screen a card offers Update & restart.

There are two jars: magmaclient-1.21.1-…jar for NeoForge and magmaclient-fabric-1.21.1-…jar for Fabric (needs Fabric API). Put only one Magma jar in your mods folder.
